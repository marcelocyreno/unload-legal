Privacy Policy

Effective 1 September 2026 · Last updated 1 September 2026

UnLoad is a voice note app. You record a thought, and the app transcribes it, summarises it and pulls out any action items. This policy explains exactly what happens to that recording and to everything derived from it.

It covers the UnLoad mobile app (Android package com.cyreno.unload) and the transcription service it talks to. UnLoad is made by Marcelo Cyreno ("we", "us"), who is the controller of the limited data described below.

The short version.

Your notes live on your phone. There is no account and no sign-in. We do not use ads, analytics or third-party trackers, and we never sell or share your data. Audio is sent to our service only to be transcribed, and is discarded the moment the transcript comes back — we keep no copy of your audio, your transcripts, your summaries or your tasks.

1. What stays on your device

Everything UnLoad produces is stored in the app's private storage on your phone, where no other app can read it:

  • your audio recordings (.m4a files);
  • the notes index — transcripts, summaries, action items and topics;
  • your language preference;
  • an install identifier (see below).

None of this is backed up to us or synced to any server. If you lose the device or uninstall the app, we cannot recover any of it, because we never had it.

2. What leaves your device, and why

When a recording finishes, the app sends it over an encrypted HTTPS connection to our transcription service, a Cloudflare Worker running Cloudflare Workers AI. The service transcribes the audio, summarises the transcript, and returns the result to your phone.

About the install identifier

On first use, the app generates a random identifier and saves it in a file on your device. It is not derived from your phone, your hardware, your advertising ID or anything about you — it is a random string that identifies an installation, not a person. It is not shared with anyone, and it is not used for advertising or profiling. Deleting and reinstalling the app produces a new one.

3. What we log

Our service keeps ordinary operational logs so that failures can be diagnosed. A log line records the type of event, the install and note identifiers, the size of the upload in bytes, how long the request took, and any error message. Logs are held by Cloudflare on our behalf and are retained for a short period under Cloudflare's own log retention.

Logs never contain your audio, your transcript, your summary or your action items.

4. Permissions the app asks for

  • Microphone — the only way to record a voice note. UnLoad records only while you are actively recording, never in the background, and never without you starting it.
  • Network access — to send a recording for transcription and receive the result.

5. What we do not do

  • No accounts, no sign-in, no passwords. The "Sign in with Google" control in the app is not connected to anything yet: it only opens a dialog explaining what sign-in would bring in a future version. It sends no data anywhere.
  • No advertising, no ad networks, no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDKs, and no third-party trackers of any kind.
  • No selling or sharing of personal information, in the sense of the GDPR, the LGPD, or the CCPA/CPRA.
  • No use of your recordings, transcripts or notes to train AI models. Cloudflare Workers AI processes your audio to produce your result and does not use customer inputs to train its models.

6. Your choices and your rights

  • Delete a note — deleting a note in the app deletes its recording along with it.
  • Delete everything — uninstalling UnLoad removes all of its data from your device. Since we hold no copy, that is a complete erasure.
  • Withdraw microphone access — you can revoke it at any time in Android Settings. UnLoad cannot record without it.

If you are in the EU/EEA or the UK, our processing of the identifiers in section 2 rests on our legitimate interest in keeping the service available and debuggable (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR); processing your audio to produce your transcript is necessary to provide the service you asked for (Article 6(1)(b)). If you are in Brazil, the equivalent bases under the LGPD apply. Because we store nothing tied to you, requests to access, correct, port or erase data held by us will normally be answered by confirming that we hold none. You may still contact us, and you have the right to complain to your local data protection authority — in Brazil, the ANPD.

7. Children

UnLoad is not directed to children and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will act on it.

8. International transfers

Our service runs on Cloudflare's global network, so a recording is processed at whichever Cloudflare location is nearest to you and may be processed outside your country. Because the audio is not stored, the transfer lasts only as long as the request. Cloudflare acts as our processor under its own data processing terms.

9. Security

All traffic between the app and our service is encrypted in transit with TLS. On-device data is held in the app's private storage, protected by Android's app sandbox and by your device's own encryption. No system is perfect, but the strongest protection here is structural: there is no server-side store of your notes to breach.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially — in particular if a future version of UnLoad adds accounts, sync or backup — we will update this page and change the "last updated" date above before the change takes effect, and where the change is significant we will say so in the app.
